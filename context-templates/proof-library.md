**Permission levels**
- `public`: may appear anywhere.
- `named in 1:1 only`: may be named in private sales conversations and documents, never in public marketing.
- `anonymized only`: describe the customer only in a way that does not identify them.
- `internal only`: sales may know it; never put it in customer-facing copy.

**Status:** `active` items may be used within their permission level. `expired` items must never be used, in any context.
