# Strategic Account Plan (Tier 1 / Expansion)

Use this for named Tier-1 targets and for growing existing enterprise customers across business units, brands or regions. Keep it to roughly 2–4 pages. It is a working document, not a report.

```
# <Account> — Account Plan · v<n> · <date> · Owner: <AE> · Exec sponsor: <name>

## 1. Account thesis (5 lines max)
Why this account matters to us, what we believe they need, and the size of the opportunity — each claim labeled Confirmed / Reported / Inferred / Unknown.

## 2. Their world
- Strategic priorities (quotes + source)
- Key initiatives and owners
- Pressures (financial, competitive, regulatory) — sourced
- Org structure for our domain (BUs, brands, regions, central vs. decentralized buying)

## 3. Whitespace map
| Business unit / brand / region | Current state (customer? product? spend?) | Need hypothesis | Owner on their side | Our relationship | Potential | Next step |

## 4. Relationship map
Committee per active opportunity (from SKILL.md §2) + executive-level relationship matrix: their exec ↔ our exec, strength, last contact.

## 5. Value delivered so far (existing customers)
Outcomes achieved, in the customer's metrics, with evidence — and gaps or issues (support, adoption, unhappy stakeholders). Do not hide problems; an expansion plan built on a shaky base fails.

## 6. Objectives (next 2–4 quarters)
3–5 objectives, each measurable, e.g., "Expand from Brand A to Brands B and C (land 1 by Q3)".

## 7. Strategies & actions
| Objective | Action | Owner | Date | Status |

## 8. Risks & mitigations
Champion departure, budget cuts, incumbent consolidation, renewal risk, security/AI policy changes, M&A.

## 9. Asks of our leadership
Executive time, resources (SE, solutions, partner), commercial flexibility — each with justification.
```

## Account health score (for existing customers)
Score each 1–5, with the evidence noted:
- **Value realized:** outcomes the customer has stated, measured against the success criteria.
- **Adoption:** depth and breadth of usage, trend.
- **Relationship:** executive sponsor engaged, champion stable, number of active relationships.
- **Commercial:** renewal date, payment, expansion trajectory.
- **Risk:** open escalations, leadership changes, competitor activity.

Average them, but always list the lowest-scoring dimension first. A 5/5/5/5/1 account is at risk.
