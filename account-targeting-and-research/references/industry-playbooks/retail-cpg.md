# Industry Playbook: Retail & CPG

These are lenses for research and messaging, not facts about any account. Treat every point as a hypothesis (**Inferred**) until the account's own filings, statements or conversations confirm it. Never quote an industry benchmark to a buyer unless it is in the proof library with a source.

## Who buys, and what they're measured on

### Selling to CPG brands (e.g., a content, data, AI or commerce vendor)
| Role | Usually measured on |
|---|---|
| CEO / CFO | Organic net revenue growth split into **price/mix vs. volume**; gross margin; gross-to-net; A&P efficiency; earnings guidance |
| Chief Customer Officer / VP Sales (key accounts) | Retailer growth vs. plan, JBP delivery, trade ROI, OTIF / fill rate, deductions |
| Revenue Growth Management (RGM) lead | Price realization, promo ROI, price-pack architecture, mix |
| VP eCommerce / Digital Commerce | Online sales and share; **digital shelf**: content compliance, search rank, ratings and reviews, in-stock / buy box; retail media ROAS / iROAS |
| CMO, brand, shopper and trade marketing | Household penetration, repeat, brand health, media effectiveness, content velocity |
| Category management and insights | Category growth and share, fair share of shelf, line-review wins |
| Supply chain / customer service | OTIF, fill rate, forecast accuracy |
| Legal, regulatory, quality | Claims substantiation, label compliance (see `customer-facing-quality-gate/references/industry-rules/cpg-product-claims.md`) |

**Common pains (to validate):**
- Retail media becoming "pay to play".
- Trade spend that doesn't pay back.
- Deductions.
- Private-label encroachment.
- One product's content maintained across many retailers.
- Retailers pushing back on price increases.
- Restrictions on using retailer data.
- SKU complexity.
- Speed of launch to shelf and to PDP.

### Selling to retailers
| Role | Usually measured on |
|---|---|
| Category manager / buyer (merchant), DMM / GMM | Category sales and share vs. market; margin % and margin $ including **back margin** (trade funds, MDF); GMROI, turns; price image (KVI index); private-label penetration |
| Replenishment / supply chain | In-stock / on-shelf availability, inventory |
| Space planning | Sales per linear foot, planogram compliance |
| eCommerce / digital merchant | Online conversion, content quality, fulfillment cost |
| Retail media network team | Retail media revenue, advertiser ROAS |
| Store operations | Labor, shrink |
| Vendor compliance | Supplier scorecards, chargebacks |

**Common pains:**
- Margin squeeze and traffic.
- Out-of-stocks and phantom inventory.
- Store labor.
- eCommerce fulfillment cost.
- SKU complexity.
- Private-label growth targets.

## How they buy
- **Vendors selling to CPG** (approval chain): business sponsor → digital or IT → security and legal → procurement. Global CPGs often run regional pilots before a global rollout, and their procurement is mature.
- **Retailer processes** (when a CPG sells to a retailer): annual JBP, line and category reviews on the retailer's own calendar, resets, and item setup.
- **Timing:**
  - Retailer Q4 and holiday freezes slow tech change for retailers and for brands' retail-facing teams.
  - Category-review calendars dictate when a brand needs new content or data.
  - **Confirm each account's calendar.**

## Research sources and triggers
- **Sources:** earnings calls, filings and investor days, plus their own PDPs across retailers.
- **Earnings-call language that signals pressure:**
  - "price/mix", "elasticity", "volume softness"
  - "RGM", "retail media"
  - "digital shelf", "innovation pipeline"
- **What to check on their PDPs** (see `abm-programs.md`, outside-in assessment): content consistency, missing attributes, image count, ratings.
- **Growth-constraint hypotheses.** Find which of these is binding:
  - distribution (%ACV, numeric vs. weighted)
  - velocity
  - innovation
  - pricing
  - supply
- **Triggers:**
  - a price increase announced, or private-label share commentary
  - a new CCO, CMO or eCommerce leader
  - M&A (portfolio and content harmonization)
  - new retailer program mandates (content standards, supplier scorecards, retail media)
  - regulation, e.g.:
    - front-of-pack and "healthy" labeling rules
    - US state packaging and EPR laws
    - PFAS bans
    - EU Directive 2024/825 on environmental claims

## Vocabulary (use it correctly)
- **Planning and shelf:** JBP · line review vs. category review · planogram (POG) / reset / facings · share of shelf / fair share · ACV / %ACV · TDP · velocity (SPPD, $/TDP) · CDI / BDI.
- **Promotions:** TPR · feature / display · BOGO / multibuy · EDLP vs. Hi-Lo.
- **Trade terms:** off-invoice · scan-down / bill-back · MDF / co-op · slotting / new-item fees · gross-to-net / net-net · trade rate.
- **Operations and supply:** deductions / chargebacks · OTIF · MABD · fill rate · ASN · EDI · DSD vs. warehouse · co-man · MOQ.
- **Pricing and category roles:** KVI · price-pack architecture (PPA) · RGM · private label / store brand · category captain / validator.
- **Retail media and data:** retail media network (RMN) · closed-loop measurement · clean room · syndicated data (Circana, NielsenIQ), panel data (Numerator) and retailer POS portals.
- **Marketplaces and digital shelf:** 1P / 3P · Vendor Central / Seller Central · A+ content · buy box.

## Sensitivities
- **Retailer-provided data** (POS, loyalty, clean-room outputs) is usable only as each agreement allows. It must never appear in another retailer's materials.
- **Never ask a brand for competitor pricing or plans,** and never pass them along. That is an antitrust risk.
- **Talk about category growth, not just brand growth,** when the audience is a retailer.
